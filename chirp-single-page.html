How your talent gets paid

Every time a brand finds one of your fans on another screen, your talent earns.

1
Brand buys access to your talent's fan audience

Through ADvolution, brands pay to reach verified fans across streaming TV, mobile games, and web — through tools they already use.

2
A fan sees the brand's ad on another screen

That Chirp subscriber is watching TV or playing a game. The brand's ad finds them there. Each time, an impression is logged.

3
The publisher pays ADvolution a data fee

When the ad wins the auction, the publisher pays out the bid. ADvolution collects a data fee — because your talent's fan data made that impression worth buying.

4
ADvolution shares that fee with your talent — automatically

Revenue flows back per impression. No invoice. No negotiation. No sponsored post required.
